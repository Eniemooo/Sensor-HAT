LCSC / EasyEDA component library data

Some symbols and footprints in lib/ were converted with easyeda2kicad
(https://github.com/uPesy/easyeda2kicad.py) from the LCSC / EasyEDA component
library. Copies of them are embedded in Sensor-HAT.kicad_sch and
Sensor-HAT.kicad_pcb, and the production files, PDFs and renders are derived
from them. Each part carries its LCSC part number.
